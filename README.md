## Favorite Movie: Barbie: A Mermaid Tale

**Barbie: A Mermaid Tale** is an animated movie about *Merliah*, a girl who loves surfing. She finds out that she is really a mermaid and has to save the ocean.

### Why I Like It
- It has a fun and magical story
- The ocean scenes are colorful
- It teaches about being brave and helping others

## Favorite Series: Gossip Girl

**Gossip Girl** is a teen drama series set in New York City. It follows rich students in the Upper East Side and the secrets that get spread around by an anonymous blogger.

### Main Characters
1. Serena van der Woodsen
2. Blair Waldorf
3. Dan Humphrey
4. Chuck Bass
5. Nate Archibald

### Why I Like It
> The drama, the fashion, and the plot twists keep me watching.
